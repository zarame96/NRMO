# NRMO Research Philosophy

NRMO is a living research system. Observation, mathematical formalization, implementation, validation and governance form a continuous cycle.

Principles:
- Human Sovereign defines Vision.
- NRMO defines governance and non-ruin boundaries.
- Engines explore only within permitted actions.
- Evidence has priority over intuition.
- Negative results are preserved as research assets.
- Implementations must not redefine theory.
