## 1. What this decision is

A **publication-identity** decision only: which build is the current
"NRMO Integrated System" publication that a new reader should treat as
authoritative. It designates v7.3 as that publication, in place of
v7.2 rev2.

**This decision does not, by itself, do any of the following** (each
remains exactly as it was before this decision, unless a separate,
explicitly cited decision changed it):

- Does not amend `NORMATIVE_CANON.md`.
- Does not delete or rewrite v7.2's historical content, including the
  preserved v7.2-era frontmatter pages (Final Build Statement, Per-Part
  Version Manifest, Publication and Provenance Note) — these remain in
  the v7.3 PDF exactly as written, now framed as historical publication
  material rather than the current one.
- Does not change the lineage of Parts I--XV, which remain preserved
  v7.2/v7.2.1-baseline content; only Part XVI is v7.3-original.
- Does not adopt Item 5b (`DEFERRED / OUT OF v7.3 NORMATIVE SCOPE ---
  NOT ADOPTED`, unchanged).
- Does not declare the DecisionCompass implementation fully conformant;
  10.1a (`NOT_VERIFIED`) and 11.1b (`PARTIAL`) are unaffected.
- Does not open, close, or otherwise act on any
  `zarame96/DecisionCompass` file.
- Does not add v7.4 content of any kind.

## 2. Reasoning

v7.3's own content readiness was already established and recorded
(`V7_3_PUBLICATION_READINESS.md`, 2026-09-22): 0 normative blockers, 0
unresolved SOURCE-PENDING rows, the located original source
byte-integrity-guarded, and PROVISIONAL status already removed
(`V7_3_PUBLICATION_DECISION.md`, same day). What remained undecided
after that record was published-**identity** status specifically —
whether v7.3, now FINAL and merged into `main`, should also be treated
as *the* current publication of the NRMO Integrated System, ahead of
v7.2 rev2. This decision resolves that question: yes, as of 2026-09-23.
