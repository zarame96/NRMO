\section{[R] Failure Log Input --- Reconstruction \& Analysis}
\label{sec:secretary-R}

Classifies failure points and outputs:

\begin{enumerate}
\item the next defensive action (1 move),
\item a retreat line, and
\item a 3-line log.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
The module \textbf{does not execute} (proposals only).

\paragraph{Input parameters.}

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{0.85\linewidth}{lX}
\toprule
\textbf{Input parameter} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
BV ($0$--$3$) & Burnout / Vulnerability Score \\
$\varepsilon^m$ & Execution-prohibition tolerance \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption[\,[R] module input parameters]{[R] module input
parameters.}
\label{tab:R-input}
\end{table}

\paragraph{Procedure (4 stages).}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{ANALYZE \& RECONSTRUCT}: identify the FATAL POINT.
\item \textbf{SURVIVAL ROUTE}: produce one defensive move plus a
retreat line.
\item \textbf{NEXT (choose 1)} and \textbf{LOG (3 lines)}.
\item \textbf{AUDIT COMMENT}: a structured note on the failure.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Pipeline (visual).}

\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\small,frame=single]
[ ANALYZE & RECONSTRUCT ]
       |
       v
   FATAL POINT
       |
       v
[ SURVIVAL ROUTE ]
       |
       +----> NEXT (choose 1)
       |
       +----> LOG (3 lines)
       |
       v
[ AUDIT COMMENT ]
\end{lstlisting}

\section{[W] Emotional Filtering --- Safe Draft Generation}
\label{sec:secretary-W}

Normalises input into a fixed structure: \textit{Subject / Facts /
Request / Options}. Removes threats, urgency, assertions, and
personal evaluations; applies a cooling template.

\paragraph{Output condition.}
Low-temperature, reversible. Do not escalate pressure.

\paragraph{Procedure.}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Normalise text (\textbf{NORMALIZE TEXT}).
\item Generate the message (\textbf{GENERATED MESSAGE}).
\item Output the cooled draft (\textbf{Output}).
\item Apply \textbf{SAFE PROTOCOL: APPLIED}.
\item \textbf{NEXT (choose 1)} and \textbf{LOG (3 lines)}.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{BV-conditioned behaviour.}

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{0.85\linewidth}{lX}
\toprule
\textbf{BV} & \textbf{Purpose} \\
\midrule
$0$ & Standard cooling. \\
$1$ & Strengthened cooling; insistence reduced. \\
$2$ & Full cooling; only minimal options preserved. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption[\,[W] BV-conditioned behaviour]{[W] BV-conditioned
behaviour.}
\label{tab:W-bv}
\end{table}

\section{[Z] Mental Detox Protocol}
\label{sec:secretary-Z}

Restores decision-making capacity. When BV is high, temporarily
locks R/W/G modules (accident prevention).

\paragraph{Lock conditions.}

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{0.85\linewidth}{lX}
\toprule
\textbf{Trigger} & \textbf{Action} \\
\midrule
$\mathrm{BV} \ge 2$ & R/W/G LOCK engaged \\
\textbf{Unlock} & \texttt{CLEAR LOCK} --- manual release when calm \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption[\,[Z] lock conditions]{[Z] lock conditions.}
\label{tab:Z-lock}
\end{table}

\paragraph{Operation sequence.}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{APPLY LOCK} if $\mathrm{BV} \ge 2$ $\to$ LOCK: ON.
\item \textbf{DUMP MEMORY CACHE} --- write out current cognitive
noise.
\item \textbf{PURGE \& RESET} --- SYSTEM PURGED.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Recovery steps (3).}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Body}: drink water / drop shoulders / breathe slowly
(choose one).
\item \textbf{Cognition}: separate ``facts'' (1 line) and
``interpretation'' (1 line).
\item \textbf{Boundary}: no decision today / defer to tomorrow /
third-party check (choose one).
\end{enumerate}

\section{[G] Governance --- Border / Agreement / Log}
\label{sec:secretary-G}

Generates boundaries, agreements, and logs. Does not execute (output
only).

\paragraph{Inputs.}
\begin{itemize}
\item BV ($0$--$3$).
\item Boundary setting (1 line).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Outputs.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Declaration (verbalisation of agreement / boundary).
\item Output of boundary, agreement, and log.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Procedure.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{GENERATE GOVERNANCE OUTPUT}.
\item \textbf{OUTPUT}: boundary, agreement, log.
\item \textbf{NEXT (choose 1)} and \textbf{LOG (3 lines)}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{[ORIENTATION] Hidden Mode --- BE $\to$ DO $\to$ HAVE}
\label{sec:secretary-orientation}

Creates a \emph{direction} to continue decision-making without
breaking, not a ``goal.'' Asks questions in BE $\to$ DO $\to$ HAVE
order; when exhausted, asks \textit{``What is your VISION?''}
\textbf{STOP is always an acceptable action}.

\paragraph{Conditions.}

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{0.95\linewidth}{lX}
\toprule
\textbf{Condition} & \textbf{Effect} \\
\midrule
$\mathrm{BV} \ge 2$ & Abbreviated mode (fatigue priority); suppress
number of questions. \\
Irreversible word detected & Warning issued during VISION evaluation
$(m)$. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption[\,[ORIENTATION] conditions]{[ORIENTATION] mode conditions.}
\label{tab:orientation-conditions}
\end{table}

\paragraph{Process.}

\begin{enumerate}
\item Press START to begin session.
\item Answer BE / DO / HAVE questions with SUBMIT.
\item SKIP / STOP are always valid (acceptable action).
\item When VISION is exhausted, display \textbf{NRMO EVALUATION}
($0$--$10$).
\item Confirm NEXT (minimal \& reversible) --- do not execute here.
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Mandala chart (\,3$\times$3 grid).}

\begin{itemize}
\item Centre = VISION.
\item Surrounding 8 blocks = BE, DO, HAVE, Non-Ruin, and other
dimensions, auto-arranged.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Irreversible-word detection.}
Words such as \textit{``absolutely,'' ``forever,'' ``bet
everything,'' ``right now''} trigger warnings during VISION
evaluation. These words signal a potential commitment to an
irreversible state.
