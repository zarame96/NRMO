\chapter{v2.1 Sovereign \texttt{+} Reflex Extension ---
Additive Only}
\label{ch:v21-extension-preamble}

\nrmobox{Extension scope}{
This chapter is an additive-only extension to v2.0 FINAL. It
contains \textbf{no changes} to the existing v2.0 structure. All
additions operate as \emph{outer layers} of the pipeline.
}

The v2.1 extension introduces five outer layers and supporting
governance:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Reflex Layer} (Chapter~\ref{ch:reflex-layer}) ---
Expert responsiveness with R1--R6 fixed-order rules.
\item \textbf{Mode Selector} (Chapter~\ref{ch:mode-selector}) ---
Reflex $\Leftrightarrow$ Institutional mode toggle via DSI.
\item \textbf{MetaGovernanceLayer}
(Chapter~\ref{ch:meta-governance}) --- Cross-domain ruin detection,
$\varepsilon$ change audit, DSI monitoring.
\item \textbf{TimeHorizonLayer}
(Chapter~\ref{ch:time-horizon-layer}) --- Five-horizon irreversibility
monitoring, display-only \texttt{warn\_flag}.
\item \textbf{Situation Parameter Model}
(Chapter~\ref{ch:situation-parameter-model}) --- 8-tuple parametric
encoding replacing 3000-case static branching.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
And supporting components:

\begin{itemize}
\item NonErgodicCumulativeMonitor
(Chapter~\ref{ch:non-ergodic-monitor}).
\item Success Definitions (Chapter~\ref{ch:success-definitions}).
\item v2.1 Integrated Execution Flow + Invariant Registry
(Chapter~\ref{ch:v21-integrated-flow}).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Constitutional principle.}
v2.0 FINAL is preserved \emph{verbatim} (semantic and structural).
v2.1 layers are appended at the boundary; they may inhibit (HALT,
HOLD, demote, immediate-stop), but they may not modify v2.0 logic.
This preservation discipline is enforced by the
\emph{Chapter Mapping Ledger} and the
\emph{Invariant Registry}, both reproduced in this Part.
