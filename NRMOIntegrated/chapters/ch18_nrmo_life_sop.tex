\section{Premise Update}
\label{sec:life-sop-premise}

\begin{itemize}
\item Life is neither a management target nor an optimisation
target.
\item Active intervention is primary; OUTER\_SHELL and CORE handle
accidents.
\item Evaluation, success conditions, and improvement are
prohibited.
\end{itemize}

% =====================================================================
\section{Morning (Startup Confirmation)}
\label{sec:life-sop-morning}

Only confirm where today's intervention possibilities are.

\begin{itemize}
\item[$\square$] Does a target come to mind where I want to reach
out to the world today? (Yes / No)
\item[$\square$] Is it curiosity-origin? (Yes / No / Unknown)
\end{itemize}

\noindent
``No'' or ``Unknown'' is also fine. Judgement and planning are
prohibited.

% =====================================================================
\section{Noon (Intervention Execution)}
\label{sec:life-sop-noon}

Small is fine. Results are not questioned. Establish the subject
only immediately before intervention.

\begin{itemize}
\item[$\square$] Pre-intervention declaration (internal, not
recorded): \textit{``This is what I do. The result returns to me.''}
\item[$\square$] Have I intervened in the world even once today?
(Yes / No)
\item[$\square$] Can I accept this as my own will? (Yes / No)
\end{itemize}

\noindent
If danger or irreversibility is detected: delegate only to
DECISION\_SURVIVAL\_ENGINE.

% =====================================================================
\section{Night (Non-Recovery)}
\label{sec:life-sop-night}

Do not assign meaning, reflect, or learn.

\begin{itemize}
\item[$\square$] Did I have the feeling today that I touched the
world as a subject? (Yes / No)
\end{itemize}

\noindent
Both ``Yes'' and ``No'' are success.

% =====================================================================
\section{Sole Failure Definition}
\label{sec:life-sop-failure}

\nrmobox{Sole failure definition}{
\centering
\textit{Today, I never once remembered that I was a subject.}
}

\noindent
(Not ``failed to intervene'' or ``got bad results'' --- only
``forgot I was a subject'' is failure.)

% =====================================================================
\section{Design Summary}
\label{sec:life-sop-design-summary}

\begin{quote}
\itshape
Life comes first; design is cleanup.\\
Active intervention is the subject; safety is the background.
\end{quote}

\noindent
This system does not make judgements. It only provides non-breaking
options.
