\section{Design Philosophy}
\label{sec:reflex-design-philosophy}

ReflexLayer is the outermost layer that wraps the entire v2.0 NRMO
pipeline. It emulates the ``intuitive refusal'' of experts, enabling
immediate HOLD issuance without passing through MetaGovernance or
NRMOSystem.

\paragraph{Design constraints.}

\begin{itemize}
\item Score-free (no quantitative weighting).
\item Simple conditional branches only (within 200 lines).
\item HoldImmediate $\to$ completely skips MetaGovernance and
NRMOSystem.
\end{itemize}

\section{Fixed-Order Rules (R1--R6)}
\label{sec:reflex-rules}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{R1}: \texttt{anomaly} AND
\texttt{time\_pressure} $\ge 8$ $\to$ \texttt{HoldImmediate}.\\
\textit{High time pressure under anomaly. Immediate stop.}

\item \textbf{R2}: \texttt{historical\_fail} AND
\texttt{anomaly} $\to$ \texttt{HoldImmediate}.\\
\textit{Similar pattern where past failure occurred. Prevents
repetition.}

\item \textbf{R3}: \texttt{emotional\_distortion} AND
\texttt{time\_pressure} $\ge 6$ $\to$ \texttt{HoldImmediate}.\\
\textit{Moderate or higher time pressure under emotional distortion.
Suspend judgement.}

\item \textbf{R4}: \texttt{ambiguity} $\ge 0.8$ AND
\texttt{historical\_fail} $\to$ \texttt{HoldImmediate}.\\
\textit{High ambiguity and past failure pattern. Refuses action with
insufficient information.}

\item \textbf{R5}: \texttt{time\_pressure} $\ge 9$ $\to$
\texttt{HoldImmediate}.\\
\textit{Fires on extreme time pressure alone. Being rushed is itself
a risk.}

\item \textbf{R6}: $\to$ \texttt{Proceed}.\\
\textit{Passes only when no conditions are triggered.}
\end{enumerate}

\nrmobox{Critical constraint}{
When \texttt{HoldImmediate} fires, MetaGovernance and NRMOSystem are
\textbf{completely skipped}. This is intentional design,
guaranteeing the ability to ``stop before thinking.''
}
