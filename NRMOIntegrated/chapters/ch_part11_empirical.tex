\paragraph{Key empirical findings.}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Adaptive\_NRMOvNext\_$\Omega$Full} achieves the
highest survival rate (61\%) and the highest score (0.4974) among
NRMO-family strategies.
\item \textbf{RiskAdjustedUtility} achieves a comparable score
(0.4369) but with higher passive-ruin rate (10\% vs.~3\%).
\item \textbf{ExpectedValueMax} demonstrates the textbook failure
mode: 95\% true ruin, score $-0.4955$.
\item Adding $\Omega$ Full on top of vNext (rows 2 vs.~5) raises
survival from 51\% to 61\% (+10 percentage points) and reduces
passive ruin from 3\% to 3\%.
\item \textbf{UltraConservative} avoids dramatic failure (lowest
final $X = 14.03$) but suffers passive-ruin pathology (7\% rate),
confirming the structural-decay hypothesis.
\end{itemize}

estimator).

exploration-floor constraint, exposes more $R$-axis failure.

NRMO\_Original, and AlphaSearch concentrate failures on
\texttt{environment\_collapse} (active overshoot). UltraConservative
fails almost exclusively via \texttt{resource\_collapse} from
under-investment in growth.
\item \textbf{$\Omega$ Full reduces total ruin count by 18\%}
(39 vs.~48 of NRMOvNext\_StrongEngine).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Ablation observations.}

\begin{itemize}
\item At horizon 200, $\Omega$ Full and RiskAdjustedUtility tie
across all three $\lambda$ settings. The drift penalty is invisible
at this scale.
\item At horizon 1000, $\Omega$ Full beats RiskAdjustedUtility by
7~percentage points across all three $\lambda$ settings. The
drift-penalty mechanism activates only on long horizons.
\item Absence of $\lambda$-sensitivity within the swept range
($0.8$--$1.2$) suggests the penalty is operating in a saturated
regime; deeper sensitivity studies (e.g., $\lambda \in \{0.0, 0.5,
1.0, 2.0, 5.0\}$) are deferred.
\end{itemize}

\section{Empirical Status: Honest Reporting}
\label{sec:empirical-honest-status}

\nrmobox{Empirical claim status (per README.md)}{
$\Omega$ Full exists as a documented final execution-layer
architecture. Smoke validation (1{,}500 episodes) is complete.
\textbf{Full-scale empirical superiority is NOT claimed.}
}

\paragraph{Computational status.}

\begin{itemize}
\item Smoke test (1{,}500 episodes, $n = 20$ each cell) is the
\emph{maximum} validated scale at this point.
\item Main experiment design (25{,}000 episodes, $n = 500$ each
cell) is specified but not executed (computational bottleneck).
\item Cross-strategy ranking is suggestive but not statistically
conclusive at $n = 20$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Validated claims.}

\begin{itemize}
\item Survival-rate improvement of $\Omega$ Full over baseline SE:
\textbf{+10 percentage points} (61\% vs.~51\%) cross-world.
\item Score improvement: \textbf{+0.16} (0.497 vs.~0.338) on the
composite metric.
\item True-ruin-rate reduction: $-10$ percentage points (39\%
vs.~49\%).
\item Drift-control mechanism activates at horizon 1000 with
$+7$~percentage-point survival improvement over RiskAdjustedUtility.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Known weaknesses (planned remedies).}

\begin{itemize}
\item Vulnerable world: 5\% survival ceiling for $\Omega$ Full
($+5\%$ over baseline SE). Plan: enrich rollout-shock model.
\item FastExpansionRace world: 35\% survival, vs.~50\% for
RiskAdjustedUtility. Plan: rivalry-sensitive drift estimator
(implemented in v5.2).
\item Smoke-only $n = 20$: full-scale validation deferred.
\end{itemize}
