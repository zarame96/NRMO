\section{CrossDomainRuin Detection}
\label{sec:cross-domain-ruin}

MetaGovernanceLayer monitors systemic risk across seven domains:

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\small
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{cXX}
\toprule
\textbf{Index} & \textbf{Domain} & \textbf{Content} \\
\midrule
0 & Financial & Finance and assets. \\
1 & Health & Health and body. \\
2 & Career & Career and occupation. \\
3 & Relationship & Relationships. \\
4 & Legal & Legal risk. \\
5 & Reputation & Reputation. \\
6 & Infrastructure & Life infrastructure. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption[Cross-domain risk monitoring]{Seven domains monitored by
MetaGovernanceLayer for systemic risk.}
\label{tab:cross-domain-domains}
\end{table}

\begin{equation}
\mathrm{systemic\_risk} =
\frac{1}{7} \sum_{i=0}^{6} \mathrm{domain\_risk}_i.
\label{eq:systemic-risk}
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Trigger.}

\begin{equation}
\mathrm{systemic\_risk} \ge 0.75 \;\Rightarrow\; \mathrm{SAFE\_EXIT}.
\label{eq:cross-domain-trigger}
\end{equation}

\section{$\varepsilon$ Change Audit}
\label{sec:meta-epsilon-audit}

$\varepsilon$ changes are recorded in an append-only log:

\begin{itemize}
\item Signed appends only (overwriting prohibited).
\item Stored in persistent storage.
\item Recording reason for change is mandatory.
\end{itemize}
