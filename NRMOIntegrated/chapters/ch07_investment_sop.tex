\section{Scope and Basic Principles}
\label{sec:invest-scope}

\begin{description}
\item[Survival First] Avoid unrecoverable collapse as the top
priority.
\item[Anti-Ergodicity] Optimise time-average survival, not
ensemble-average expected value.
\item[Optionality] Maintain reversibility --- the ability to redo,
exit, or rebalance.
\item[Separation] Separate theory, judgement, and execution.
\end{description}

% =====================================================================
\section{Ruin Management}
\label{sec:invest-ruin}

In this SOP, ``Ruin'' does \textbf{not} mean capital bankruptcy alone.
It refers to:

\begin{quote}
\centering
\textit{the state in which rationality for taking risk is lost.}
\end{quote}

Two grades:

\begin{description}
\item[Hard Ruin] Unrecoverable: bankruptcy, permanent capital
dilution, forced sale of all risk assets.
\item[Soft Ruin] Strategic failure: transition to Risk-Off mode and
inability to take new risk for an extended period.
\end{description}

% =====================================================================
\section{Decision Priority}
\label{sec:invest-priority}

The lexicographic priority for any investment decision:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Survival constraint (ruin avoidance).
\item Reversibility.
\item Time average.
\item Short-term expected value.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Asset Management: Benchmark-Based TWR}
\label{sec:invest-twr}

\subsection{Terms}
\label{sec:invest-terms}

\begin{description}
\item[$m$] monthly index.
\item[$V_m$] end-of-month portfolio market value (USD).
\item[$\mathrm{CF}_m$] net cash in/out for the month (USD).
\item[$I_m^B$] benchmark index value at end of month $m$ (USD).
\end{description}

\subsection{Benchmark Priority}
\label{sec:invest-benchmark}

\begin{enumerate}
\item ACWI Total Return (USD).
\item ACWI Price (USD) + estimated dividends.
\item MSCI World Total Return (USD).
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
If none of the above is obtainable in a given month: suspend
judgement; do \textbf{not} trigger Caution or Ruin based on
incomplete data.

\subsection{Monthly Time-Weighted Return}
\label{sec:invest-monthly-twr}

\begin{equation}
R_{P,m} = \frac{V_m - (V_{m-1} + \mathrm{CF}_m)}{V_{m-1} + \mathrm{CF}_m},
\qquad
R_{B,m} = \frac{I_m^B - I_{m-1}^B}{I_{m-1}^B}.
\end{equation}

\subsection{4-Month TWR --- Intermediate Judgement}
\label{sec:invest-4m-twr}

\begin{equation}
R_P^{4m}(t) = \prod_{k=t-3}^{t} (1 + R_{P,k}) - 1,
\qquad
R_B^{4m}(t) = \prod_{k=t-3}^{t} (1 + R_{B,k}) - 1.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Caution trigger.}
$R_P^{4m}(t) < R_B^{4m}(t)$.

\noindent
Under Caution:
\begin{itemize}
\item New risk assets: maximum $50\%$ allocation.
\item Dream / high-$\beta$ assets: new entry prohibited.
\item Existing positions: maintain.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Caution release.}
$R_P^{4m}(t) \ge R_B^{4m}(t)$.

\subsection{12-Month TWR --- Ruin Judgement}
\label{sec:invest-12m-twr}

\begin{equation}
R_P^{12m}(t) = \prod_{k=t-11}^{t} (1 + R_{P,k}) - 1,
\qquad
R_B^{12m}(t) = \prod_{k=t-11}^{t} (1 + R_{B,k}) - 1.
\end{equation}

\paragraph{Risk-Off trigger.}
$R_P^{12m}(t) < R_B^{12m}(t)$.

\noindent
Under Risk-Off:
\begin{itemize}
\item New equity purchases: halted.
\item Additional funds: bonds or cash only.
\item Forced selling: \textbf{not} performed.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Risk-On Return Conditions}
\label{sec:invest-risk-on}

\begin{enumerate}
\item $R_P^{12m}(t) \ge R_B^{12m}(t)$ for three consecutive months.
\item Normal operation (CORE mode) restored.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Cash and Liquidity}
\label{sec:invest-liquidity}

\begin{itemize}
\item Maintain minimum six-month living expenses in cash at all
times.
\item Emergency reserve: separate account, not subject to TWR
calculation.
\item In Risk-Off state: no cash allocation to equities.
\end{itemize}

% =====================================================================
\section{Position Limits with Time Rules}
\label{sec:invest-position-limits}

\begin{itemize}
\item Single name: maximum $20\%$ of total investable assets.
\item Dream allocation: maximum $5\%$ of total assets.
\item New position establishment: minimum $24$-hour cooling-off
after decision.
\item Average-down prohibited when position is down more than $30\%$
from entry.
\end{itemize}

% =====================================================================
\section{Exception Handling}
\label{sec:invest-exceptions}

\nrmobox{Exception structure}{
Exceptions are \textbf{structurally prohibited}. If an exception
feeling arises, it is a MISSION / SHUTDOWN signal.
}

% =====================================================================
\section{Log}
\label{sec:invest-log}

Record monthly: TWR result, mode (CORE / CAUTION / RISK-OFF), any
special events that influenced the calculation, and any
data-availability gaps.

% =====================================================================
\section{Update Policy}
\label{sec:invest-update}

This SOP is updated \textbf{only} when NRMO theory changes.
Operational judgement does not alter this SOP. Discretionary updates
are forbidden.

\section*{Connections to Other Parts}

\begin{itemize}
\item Caution and Risk-Off triggers map to Type ZERO modes
(Section~\ref{sec:type-zero-modes-c2}): Caution corresponds to
MISSION (boundary control), Risk-Off corresponds to SHUTDOWN-
equivalent suspension of new risk acquisition.
\item Position limits implement the $A_{\mathrm{allowed}}$ caps under
the investment domain
(Section~\ref{sec:a-allowed-concrete-matrix}).
\item The benchmark-based TWR mechanism is an instance of the robust
chance-constrained control of
Section~\ref{sec:foundations-mech-b-constraint}: the benchmark
substitutes for the unknown true distribution, and the
under-performance gap substitutes for the ambiguity-set radius.
\item In the simulator (Part~VIII), the investment SOP corresponds
loosely to portfolio-domain agents, though the simulator works in
abstract civilisation state and does not implement asset-class
specifics.
\end{itemize}
