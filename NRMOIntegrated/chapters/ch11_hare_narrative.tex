\chapter{Hare-no-Hi: Narrative Random Generator}
\label{ch:hare-no-hi-narrative}

\nrmobox{Rules and sacredness}{
\textbf{Rules}: This is a festival narrative. It does not handle
correctness. Do not decide / do not carry over / erase when done
($=$ do not use as material for tomorrow's decisions).\\[0.3em]
\textbf{Sacred}: ``Sacred'' is treated not as truth-making but as a
demarcated festival space (ends / does not decide / does not carry
over).
}

\section{Small Hare Ritual (Optional)}
\label{sec:narrative-small-ritual}

Say aloud before generating:

\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\small,frame=single]
Today is Hare.
This is a festival.
I will not judge.
I will not decide.
When it ends, I return.
\end{lstlisting}

\noindent
Saying this aloud makes it easier to enter ``narrative mode.''

% =====================================================================
\section{Usage Notes}
\label{sec:narrative-usage}

\begin{itemize}
\item Selecting a stage biases location (stage expression) toward
that category.
\item Entering keywords mixes them into the narrative naturally.
\item Generated narratives are festival artefacts: do not use as
material for tomorrow's decisions.
\item The narrative output space here represents the generated story
placeholder.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Connections to Other Parts}
