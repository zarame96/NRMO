\section{Phase 4: v2.5 Production (April 2026)}
\label{sec:session-log-phase-4}

\subsection*{Lineage v1 $\to$ v2.0 $\to$ v2.1 $\to$ v2.5}

\begin{itemize}
\item v1: Initial port from v5.2 baseline. Physics drift discovered
(non-aligned transition coefficients).
\item v2.0 (April 22, 2026): Physics drift fixed by aligning
\texttt{core/state.py} and \texttt{core/worlds.py} to v5.2
baseline. Verified deterministic-part match to 0.000000.
\item v2.1: MAPLayer thresholds re-calibrated (soft = 0.10,
medium = 0.20, hard = 0.35, race = 0.15) to match observed
\texttt{danger\_confidence} values. Survival improvement
across all worlds.
\item v2.5 (April 25, 2026): Single-line PROBE penalty
\texttt{prod\_w = 0.92} added. FER survival
$50\% \to 60\%$ ($+10$ pt).
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Option C Adapter Experiment}

\begin{itemize}
\item Hypothesis: v5.2 $\Omega$ Full coupling produces effects lost
in v2.1's piecemeal port.
\item Test: \texttt{engine/omega\_v52\_adapter.py} with three
variants C1 / C2 / C3.
\item Concerns resolved before drawing conclusions:
monkey-patch effectiveness verified; RNG distribution correct;
transition exact match.
\item Decisive measurement at $n = 50$: C1 (adapter alone) provides
no benefit; C3's gain comes from the score patch itself, not from
v5.2 internals.
\item Conclusion: $\Omega$ Full coupling hypothesis from
SESSION\_HANDOFF-7 \S 8 is not supported.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{C3 $\to$ v2.5 Transplant}

\begin{itemize}
\item Source-attribution test: disabling Recovery patch in C3 drops
FER back to 48\%.
\item Insight: v2.1 already has mode-aware productivity weights for
BUFFER and RETREAT, but PROBE was implicit 1.00.
\item Action: add \texttt{prod\_w = 0.92} to PROBE branch (one-line
diff).
\item Result: FER $50 \to 60$, Vulnerable $24 \to 26$, overall best
of three; no measurable downsides.
\end{itemize}

\section{Cross-Phase Methodological Lessons}
\label{sec:session-log-cross-phase}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Constitutional discipline beats tactical scores}.
The 0.55-violation result was rejected; the 0.50-constitutional
result accepted. The 0.16 score gap was paid in exchange for
architectural integrity.

\item \textbf{Single-line changes outperform architectural
changes}. The v2.5 PROBE penalty is one line; it produced the
$+10$-pt FER improvement that the elaborate Option-C adapter
experiment failed to find.

\item \textbf{Honest measurement methodology}. The v2.5 session
caught its own measurement errors during C1 / C2 / C3 comparisons.
The chunked-run methodology with explicit env-var control and JSON
checkpointing became standard.

\item \textbf{Source-fidelity over inference}. The reconstruction
sessions consistently preferred raw source material
(\texttt{NRMO\_v5.tex}, smoke CSVs, SESSION\_HANDOFF .md) over
inference-based content. When the OCR pass yielded 134 of 200 test
cases, the user was asked to provide the source rather than
having Claude infer the missing 66.

\item \textbf{Frozen files protect invariants}.
\texttt{governance/}, \texttt{core/state.py}, and
\texttt{core/worlds.py} are explicitly do-not-change. Engine
changes are confined to \texttt{engine/} and
\texttt{strategies/}.

\item \textbf{Statistical honesty}. v2.5 explicitly reports
$z = 1.01$ ($p \approx 0.16$) for its FER $+10$-pt finding and
refuses to claim publication-grade significance.
\end{enumerate}
