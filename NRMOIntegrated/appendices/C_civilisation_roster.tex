\chapter{30-Civilisation Casebook --- Full Narratives}
\label{app:civilisation-roster}

\nrmobox{Document scope}{
This appendix provides full narrative profiles for all 30
civilisations in the casebook. Part~IX
(Section~\ref{sec:omega-priority-profiles}) gave detailed profiles
for the 10 priority civilisations; this appendix completes the
casebook by adding narrative profiles for the remaining 20.\\[0.3em]
\textbf{Source}: \texttt{NRMO\_Integrated\_Spec\_v2.pdf}
Appendix~C (full 30-civilisation roster) plus Section~18
(Casebook Design) collapse and flourishing modes, expanded with
canonical narrative arcs consistent with the v5.5 monograph
framework.
}

The 30-civilisation casebook is organised into four groups:

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{A-group (10 civilisations)}: Unbounded Growth
strategy. Active-ruin failure modes dominate.
\item \textbf{B-group (5 civilisations)}: Ultra-Conservative
strategy. Passive-ruin failure modes dominate.
\item \textbf{C-group (5 civilisations)}: Original NRMO. Robust
survival; opportunity loss.
\item \textbf{D-group (10 civilisations)}: NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$
Full default stack. Successful adaptive runs.
\end{itemize}

\section{Group A --- Unbounded Growth (Active Ruin)}
\label{sec:civ-group-a}

\subsection{A1 Sky Furnace}
\label{civ:a1}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Unbounded Growth.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Over-acceleration collapse.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Capacity expansion outpaces governance throughout the
high-growth phase; institutional slack is consumed first, then
environmental buffer; final-stage cascade precipitated by failure
of $G$ to mediate exposure shocks. Typical example of active ruin.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{environment\_collapse}
($E < 8$).

\subsection{A2 Glass Orbit}
\label{civ:a2}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Unbounded Growth, frontier-aggressive.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Frontier-exploration fragility collapse.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
High exploration rate combined with no veto floor causes
runaway $K$ growth without compensating governance investment.
Frontier projects multiply uncontrollably; when a tail event hits,
the network of exposed projects fails simultaneously.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{exposure\_cascade}
($X > 92$).

\subsection{A3 Iron Bloom}
\label{civ:a3}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Unbounded Growth, military-industrial.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Military-industrial overextension.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Allocation tilts heavily toward growth and (within that)
toward conflict-related expansion. Rivalry feedback pushes $X$
upward despite institutional safeguards. Coordination cost rises
faster than $G$ can be repaired.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{exposure\_cascade}
combined with \texttt{governance\_failure}.

\subsection{A4 Market Comet}
\label{civ:a4}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Unbounded Growth, financial.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Systemic financial failure.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Dense connectivity amplifies hidden ruin exposure: small
shocks cascade through tightly-coupled systems. The civilisation
survives many small perturbations but fails catastrophically when a
single tail event activates the full network.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{exposure\_cascade}.

\subsection{A5 Wild Archive}
\label{civ:a5}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Unbounded Growth, knowledge-rich.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Knowledge--governance separation collapse.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
$K$ accumulates rapidly but without commensurate $G$
investment. Knowledge cannot be converted into stabilising
institutions; eventually $G$ floor breaches even though knowledge
stock is high.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{governance\_failure}.

\subsection{A6 Neon Flood}
\label{civ:a6}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Unbounded Growth + low coordination cost.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Information disorder.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Information production outpaces coordination capacity. $K$
high, but signal-to-noise collapses in decision-making channels.
Effective $G$ degrades despite nominal $G$ stable; the
civilisation makes structural decisions on degraded information.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{governance\_failure}
preceded by extended drift in mean $G$.

\subsection{A7 Salt Empire}
\label{civ:a7}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Unbounded Growth, ecological extraction.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Ecological extraction trap.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Resources expand via direct $E$ depletion: $\Delta R$ funded
out of $\Delta E$ explicitly. Initial growth phase looks
spectacular; mean-reversion of $E$ then accelerates because the
buffer is exhausted. Recovery is impossible from extraction-trap
state.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{environment\_collapse}
($E < 8$).

\subsection{A8 Apex Relay}
\label{civ:a8}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Unbounded Growth + network specialisation.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Network interdependence shock.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Interdependent specialisation of subsystems; high productivity
in steady state, but small shocks propagate through the entire
network. A single $E$ shock or coordination-cost shock terminates
multiple subsystems simultaneously.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{exposure\_cascade}.

\subsection{A9 Ember Republic}
\label{civ:a9}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Unbounded Growth, reform-volatile.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Reform volatility collapse.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Periodic institutional reforms attempted to redirect growth,
but each reform produces $G$ oscillations. Lack of amendment
sandbox means each reform is an uncontrolled experiment. Eventually
a reform attempt fails during a shock window.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{governance\_failure}.

\subsection{A10 Black Sail}
\label{civ:a10}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Unbounded Growth + conquest expansion.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Conquest overreach.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
External expansion drives $\Delta R$, but rivalry feedback
escalates $X$ faster than $G$ can buffer. The conquest phase
extends the lifespan briefly; the civilisation collapses in a tail
shock once expansion can no longer compensate.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{exposure\_cascade}.

\section{Group B --- Ultra-Conservative (Passive Ruin)}
\label{sec:civ-group-b}

\subsection{B1 Stone Nest}
\label{civ:b1}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Ultra-Conservative, agrarian baseline.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Stable agrarian duration; eventual decline.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Long stable phase. Growth is minimal; environment is preserved.
Eventual decline arrives via $K$-floor breach as exploration is
suppressed for many steps. This is the textbook
\texttt{passive\_knowledge\_stagnation} signature.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.}
\texttt{passive\_knowledge\_stagnation}.

\subsection{B2 Quiet Wall}
\label{civ:b2}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Ultra-Conservative, defensive.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Passive ruin via stagnation.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
After long stability, decline of innovation leads to strategic
obsolescence. The civilisation retains physical resources but
loses optionality: $O$ drops below the threshold for 14
consecutive steps without recovery, terminating in
\texttt{passive\_optionality\_stagnation}.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.}
\texttt{passive\_optionality\_stagnation}.

\subsection{B3 Sacred Loop}
\label{civ:b3}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Ultra-Conservative + doctrinal lock-in.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Doctrinal rigidity.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Strong institutional fixation prevents amendment-sandbox
testing. When the world parameters drift (e.g., new shock regime),
the doctrinal civilisation cannot adjust its veto thresholds. $G$
remains nominally high but informationally hollow.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{passive\_compound\_decline}.

\subsection{B4 Winter Court}
\label{civ:b4}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Ultra-Conservative + aristocratic
inertia.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Aristocratic inertia decline.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Hierarchical governance preserves $G$ at high values but
suppresses exploration through formal channels. Knowledge stock
$K$ slowly degrades due to lack of recombination. The civilisation
appears strong until a sudden compound decline.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.} \texttt{passive\_compound\_decline}.

\subsection{B5 Silent Delta}
\label{civ:b5}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Ultra-Conservative + isolationist.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Slow optionality exhaustion.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Isolation suppresses external rivalry pressure but also
external opportunity. $O$ erodes as recombination channels close;
once $O$ is below the threshold, recovery requires re-opening
which the civilisation has structurally rejected.
\paragraph{Primary ruin cause.}
\texttt{passive\_optionality\_stagnation}.

\section{Group C --- Original NRMO}
\label{sec:civ-group-c}

\subsection{C1 Shield Archive}
\label{civ:c1}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Original NRMO + greedy scoring.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Standard NRMO survival.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Robust and capable. Original NRMO veto prevents active ruin;
greedy scoring handles ordinary trade-offs. Misses some frontier
opportunities (no exploration floor; no $\Omega$ search), but
survives with moderate growth across most world families.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Survives at horizon 200 in most worlds;
horizon-1000 weakness in stagnation regimes.

\subsection{C2 Harbor Logic}
\label{civ:c2}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Original NRMO, externally trade-focused.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Opportunity-missing trade pattern.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Maintains stable trade relations and hence stable $R$. Without
exploration floor, learning rate $K$ stagnates over long horizons.
Survives well in Normal world but loses ground in
LateStagnation.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Conservative survival.

\subsection{C3 Granite Compass}
\label{civ:c3}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Original NRMO + institutional emphasis.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Over-conservative institution.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Heavy investment in institutions ($G$) and safety ($s$). Stable
but cultural adaptability narrows. Identical in surface to D-group
on Normal worlds; in adaptive worlds (FastExpansionRace), the lack
of vNext exploration floor handicaps adaptation.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Stable institutional civilisation.

\subsection{C4 Lantern Chain}
\label{civ:c4}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Original NRMO + knowledge accumulation.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Knowledge without frontier.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Accumulates $K$ through within-domain learning. Without an
exploration floor, novel-frontier $K$ does not grow; vulnerable to
regime shifts where existing $K$ is no longer sufficient.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Knowledge-rich but adaptation-narrow.

\subsection{C5 Delta Guard}
\label{civ:c5}

\paragraph{Strategy.} Original NRMO + environmental priority.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Environmental resilience, muted adaptation.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Preserves $E$ above safe threshold via heavy $s$ allocation.
The eco-priority limits growth; in resource-poor worlds, $R$ floor
becomes the binding constraint instead. Survives in PlanetaryStress
but loses in FastExpansionRace.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Eco-resilient survival.

\section{Group D --- NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full (Default Stack)}
\label{sec:civ-group-d}

\subsection{D1 Open Bastion}
\label{civ:d1}

\paragraph{Strategy.} NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Baseline successful default stack.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Safe experimentation; recurring learning cycles; durable
growth. $\Omega$ Full's Wolf Pursuit efficiently captures frontier
opportunity in favourable states; Edge Survival Guard activates in
fragile states. Long-Horizon Drift Control prevents environmental
overshoot.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Canonical successful run.

\subsection{D2 Tidal Library}
\label{civ:d2}

\paragraph{Strategy.} NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full,
knowledge-emphasising.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Anti-stagnation knowledge accumulation.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
The exploration-floor invariant guarantees minimum $K$
investment per step. $K$ grows steadily over long horizons;
LateStagnation world is solved fully (100\% survival). Productivity
moderate; lifespan maximal.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Anti-stagnation specialist.

\subsection{D3 Aurora Accord}
\label{civ:d3}

\paragraph{Strategy.} NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full + diversity
maintenance.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Diversity as adaptive reserve.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Multiple sub-systems with diverse profiles. $\Omega$ Full's
Portfolio Synergy module identifies complementary candidate sets.
Recurring crises distributed across subsystems; no single failure
becomes terminal.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Distributed-resilience civilisation.

\subsection{D4 Iron Harbor}
\label{civ:d4}

\paragraph{Strategy.} NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full +
security-balance.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Security--exploration balance.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Security investment ($d$) and exploration ($l$) balanced via
mode-conditioned tuning. $G$ stays high; $K$ grows steadily.
Survives high-rivalry FastExpansionRace better than typical D-group.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Security-resilient adaptive.

\subsection{D5 Verdant Relay}
\label{civ:d5}

\paragraph{Strategy.} NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full + bio-circular.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Biosurvival circular resilience.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Resource flow modelled as circular; safeguards $s$ and
distribution $d$ form a recovery loop. $E$ recovers over time
even after shocks; PlanetaryStress survival particularly strong.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Eco-circular survival.

\subsection{D6 Prism Union}
\label{civ:d6}

\paragraph{Strategy.} NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full + federated
structure.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Federated resilience.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Federated redundancy. Local failures stay local. $\Omega$
Full's Failure Memory module learns the per-region failure
patterns; subsequent periods avoid similar failures.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Federated-redundant civilisation.

\subsection{D7 Ember Compass}
\label{civ:d7}

\paragraph{Strategy.} NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full +
high-exploration.
\paragraph{Outcome.} High-exploration with hard stops.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Higher exploration rate (above the floor) when state permits;
hard veto when $X$ rises. The strategy resembles a venture mode
with strict downside control.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Venture-with-veto civilisation.

\subsection{D8 Polar Engine}
\label{civ:d8}

\paragraph{Strategy.} NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full +
harsh-environment.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Harsh-environment endurance.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Native to high-shock environments. Edge Survival Guard
activates frequently; portfolio defaults to
$(\text{main}/\text{hedge}/\text{probe}) = (0.45/0.45/0.10)$ in
fragile periods. Survives at horizon 1000 even in Vulnerable.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Endurance specialist.

\subsection{D9 Meridian Forum}
\label{civ:d9}

\paragraph{Strategy.} NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full + amendment
sandbox.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Deliberative governance with constitutional
sandboxing.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Each major decision passes through the amendment-cycle
sandbox: hypothesis $\to$ counterfactual test $\to$ adoption /
rejection / modification with rollback. Recurring institutional
reform without existential breakage.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Deliberative civilisation.

\subsection{D10 Lattice Dawn}
\label{civ:d10}

\paragraph{Strategy.} NRMO-vNext + $\Omega$ Full +
distributed-experimentation.
\paragraph{Outcome.} Distributed local experiments + centralised
ruin constraint.
\paragraph{Narrative arc.}
Many local experiments operate in parallel; central NRMO
boundary protects against systemic ruin. Long-term knowledge
accumulation. $\Omega$ Full's Drift Control prevents cumulative
environmental degradation even at 1000-turn horizon.
\paragraph{Outcome class.} Best-in-class long-horizon civilisation.
