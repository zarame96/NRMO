\noindent
\textbf{Version semantics.}
The publication family/version is \textbf{NRMO Integrated System v7.2}.
The current integrated source is \textbf{v7.2 revision 2 (rev2)}, reflecting
the inclusion of Part~XV. The master filename
\texttt{NRMO\_Complete\_v5\_5.tex} is a retained source-lineage/build-root
name and does not define the publication version. Publication version,
Part/source baseline, normative status, and implementation/experiment
version are separate dimensions.

\noindent
Parts~I--XIII retain earlier baseline lineage, but they must not be described
as literally source-unchanged in the current tree: the 2026-09 Issue~\#6
consistency repair introduced targeted normative, historical-provenance,
implementation-provenance, and mechanical-consistency edits while preserving
historical traceability. These repairs do not silently rewrite the underlying
research lineage; they determine which meanings are controlling now.

\noindent
\textbf{NRMO naming convention} (introduced v6.4, see
Section~\ref{sec:renaming-v64}):
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{NRMO\_Origin}: original veto-only formulation
  (preserved baseline lineage), 4-rule fixed-threshold;
\item \texttt{NRMO}: strengthened operational/research form used in later
  Parts and implementations, subject to the current Normative Canon;
\item \texttt{NRMO\_Collective}: v7.0/v7.1 sibling theory incorporating
  collective governance and Collective Strong Engine development;
\item \texttt{NRMO\_vNext} (output label): retained as a strategy
  identifier in simulator outputs for backward compatibility;
\item \texttt{Loom v3.1}: Part~XIV Behavioral Core from the v7.2 research
  culmination, paired with the Sociable Shadow observation layer;
\item \texttt{Universal Adapter Framework}: Part~XV, added in v7.2 rev2,
  providing the domain-independent adapter/Hybrid layer around the core.
\end{itemize}
