pages that follow this notice were written for and describe

contains the entire v7.2 rev2 text plus one new Part.\\[0.5em]

located when this Part was first drafted --- was subsequently supplied
by the Human Sovereign, authenticity-verified, and stored at
\texttt{NRMOIntegrated/source/v7.3/original/} (2026-09-21; see
\texttt{docs/V7\_3\_ORIGINAL\_SOURCE\_DISCOVERY\_RECORD.md}). Part~XVI
marks content confirmed against that original as
\textbf{ORIGINAL-CONFIRMED}, content explicitly adopted by the Human
Sovereign with no original precedent as
\textbf{HUMAN-SOVEREIGN-ADOPTED TRACE EXTENSION}. One item (StrongEngine
internal candidate-generation diversity) was explicitly placed
\textbf{DEFERRED / OUT OF v7.3 NORMATIVE SCOPE} by Human Sovereign
decision (2026-09-22) --- not rejected, preserved as an open proposal
for a future version, and not a v7.3 publication blocker.\\[0.5em]

Canon, or any preserved historical material was altered to produce
this build. Where Part~XVI found apparent tension between the v7.3
implementation-scope evidence and the Canon, the Canon controls; see
Part~XVI, \S\ref{sec:v73-typezero-disambiguation} and
\S\ref{sec:v73-ruin-disambiguation} for the two cases found.\\[0.5em]
