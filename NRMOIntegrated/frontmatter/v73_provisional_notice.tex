\nrmobox{Read this before the v7.2 material below}{
The final-build statement, version manifest, and publication-provenance
pages that follow this notice were written for and describe
\textbf{NRMO Integrated System v7.2, Revision 2}. They are preserved
here \emph{unmodified} because this v7.3 build is additive: it
contains the entire v7.2 rev2 text plus one new Part.\\[0.5em]
\textbf{What is new in this build}: Part~\ref{part:v73_production_contract}
(``NRMO v7.3 --- Production Contract Integration''), which reconciles
the v7.2 Normative Canon and the v7.2.1 Passive Ruin revision against
the located v7.3 original source and secondary DecisionCompass
implementation-scope evidence. The original ``NRMO SYSTEM v7.3'' source
text referenced by DecisionCompass Issue \#120 --- initially not
located when this Part was first drafted --- was subsequently supplied
by the Human Sovereign, authenticity-verified, and stored at
\texttt{NRMOIntegrated/source/v7.3/original/} (2026-09-21; see
\texttt{docs/V7\_3\_ORIGINAL\_SOURCE\_DISCOVERY\_RECORD.md}). Part~XVI
marks content confirmed against that original as
\textbf{ORIGINAL-CONFIRMED}, content explicitly adopted by the Human
Sovereign with no original precedent as
\textbf{HUMAN-SOVEREIGN-ADOPTED TRACE EXTENSION}. One item (StrongEngine
internal candidate-generation diversity) was explicitly placed
\textbf{DEFERRED / OUT OF v7.3 NORMATIVE SCOPE} by Human Sovereign
decision (2026-09-22) --- not rejected, preserved as an open proposal
for a future version, and not a v7.3 publication blocker.\\[0.5em]
\textbf{What did not change}: nothing in Parts~I--XV, the Normative
Canon, or any preserved historical material was altered to produce
this build. Where Part~XVI found apparent tension between the v7.3
implementation-scope evidence and the Canon, the Canon controls; see
Part~XVI, \S\ref{sec:v73-typezero-disambiguation} and
\S\ref{sec:v73-ruin-disambiguation} for the two cases found.\\[0.5em]
\textbf{Status}: \textbf{FINAL --- PUBLICATION OF RECORD --- MAIN
INTEGRATED.} This build's specification content was PROVISIONAL from
its first draft (SOURCE-PENDING items, original source not yet
located) until 2026-09-22, when the Human Sovereign removed that
status on the basis of
\texttt{docs/V7\_3\_PUBLICATION\_READINESS.md}'s readiness assessment
(0 open normative blockers, 0 unresolved SOURCE-PENDING rows, original
source located and byte-verified, consistency guards passing; see
\texttt{docs/V7\_3\_PUBLICATION\_DECISION.md} for the decision record).
That same day the branch carrying this build was separately merged
into the repository's \texttt{main} branch via
\href{https://github.com/zarame96/NRMO/pull/16}{PR \#16} (merge commit
\texttt{cb5d2b406d4c302b1e092c619a7cf716973d1e71}), by its own separate
Human Sovereign approval. \textbf{On 2026-09-23, in a further separate
decision, the Human Sovereign designated NRMO Integrated System v7.3
the current publication of record, superseding NRMO Integrated System
v7.2 / v7.2 rev2 in that role.} v7.2 rev2 remains fully preserved in
this build as historical baseline and lineage material; see the
\emph{Publication and Provenance Note}, \emph{Final Build Statement
(v7.2)}, and \emph{Per-Part Version Manifest (v7.2 rev2)} chapters
below, each now carrying a historical-status notice. See
\texttt{NRMOIntegrated/docs/V7\_3\_DIFFERENTIAL\_TABLE.md} for the full
