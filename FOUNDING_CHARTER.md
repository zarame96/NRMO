# NRMO Research Institute — Founding Charter

## Mission
NRMO exists to research, validate, and preserve a non-ruin decision theory as a reproducible body of knowledge.

## Vision
"世界を観歩き忘れず、北極星を仰ぎ見る"

## Foundational Principles
1. NRMO Repository is the canonical source of truth.
2. The Canonical Monograph is maintained as a single publication-ready book.
3. RFCs, ADRs, White Papers, Research Notes, Validation reports, and Reference Implementations are derived artifacts and never replace the Monograph.
4. DecisionCompass is an implementation of NRMO specifications, not the source of the theory.
5. Theory evolves through observation, verification, implementation, and audit.
6. Negative results and failed experiments are preserved as research assets.

## Authority Hierarchy
Human Sovereign → Vision → NRMO → Engines → Implementations

## Long-term Goal
Publish the Canonical Monograph as the enduring record of Takashi Ikeya's research while continuing to evolve the theory through versioned editions.
